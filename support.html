欢迎使用易记

你可以用一句自然语言快速记住：

  • 物品放在哪里
  • 什么时候需要提醒
  • 临时想到的事项

当前版本支持

  • 文字录入
  • 语音录入
  • 物品位置记录
  • 提醒事项记录
  • 普通笔记记录
  • 本地搜索
  • 本地提醒通知
  • 日历天气提示（需要用户授权定位）
  • 本地备份导出与导入，可通过“文件”或 iCloud Drive 手动传递

当前版本限制

  • 暂不支持账号、group 和付费升级
  • 界面支持简体中文、繁体中文与 English
  • 语音识别语言可独立选择跟随 App、zh-CN、zh-TW 或 en-US
  • 英文可用于语音/文字笔记和关键词搜索；物品位置、提醒与时间计划的自动语义解析目前以中文为主

如何切换语言？

打开易记的“设置”页，在“语言”中分别选择 App 语言和语音识别语言。App 语言可选“跟随系统”“简体中文”“繁体中文”或“English”；语音识别可选择跟随 App，也可单独指定中国大陆普通话、台湾中文或美国英语。

常见问题

1. 为什么点击语音后没有反应？

  • 请检查是否已允许麦克风权限
  • 请检查是否已允许语音识别权限
  • 请优先在真机上测试

如需重新授权：打开 iPhone“设置”，找到“易记”，开启麦克风、语音识别和通知权限。

2. 为什么提醒没有弹出来？

  • 请检查是否已允许通知权限
  • 请确认提醒时间设置在未来
  • 请确认 iPhone 已允许易记发送系统通知

可新建一条 2～3 分钟后的提醒并锁屏等待，用真实提醒验证本地通知链路。

3. 为什么日历没有天气？

日历天气需要“使用 App 时”定位权限和可用网络。拒绝定位只会关闭天气提示，不影响记录、搜索、提醒或备份。可到 iPhone“设置”中重新授权后返回日历刷新。

4. 备份文件如何使用？

  • 在“设置”页面点击“生成备份文件”导出当前本地数据
  • 如需卸载 App 或手动转移数据，建议把备份文件保存到“文件”App 或 iCloud Drive
  • 点击“导入备份文件”恢复之前导出的 JSON 文件

请注意：导入会覆盖当前本地记录和提醒，导入后应用会重新同步本地通知。当前版本不会自动同步两台设备；跨设备使用时，请手动导出并导入备份。

5. 我的数据会上传到开发者服务器吗？

当前版本不提供账号系统或自动云同步，也不把你的记录正文、提醒内容或搜索历史上传到开发者自建服务器。语音识别能力依赖 iOS 系统能力，相关系统级处理可能受 Apple 隐私政策约束。

Welcome to Yiji

Yiji is a local-first memory app that helps you quickly save everyday information with a single natural sentence.

  • Where you put things
  • When you need a reminder
  • Notes you want to remember later

Features in the Current Version

  • Text input
  • Voice input
  • Storage and location records
  • Reminder records
  • General note records
  • Local search
  • Local reminder notifications
  • Optional calendar weather with location permission
  • Local backup export and import, with manual transfer through Files or iCloud Drive

Current Limitations

  • No account system, group sharing, or paid upgrade yet
  • The interface supports English, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese
  • Speech recognition can follow the app language or be set independently to en-US, zh-CN, or zh-TW
  • English supports voice/text notes and keyword search. Automatic item-location, reminder, and schedule parsing is currently optimized for Chinese

How do I change the language?

Open Settings in Yiji and choose the app language and speech recognition language separately. The app can follow the system or use Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, or English. Speech recognition can follow the app or use en-US, zh-CN, or zh-TW independently.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Why does voice input not start?

  • Check that microphone permission is enabled
  • Check that speech recognition permission is enabled
  • Test on a real iPhone first

To re-enable permissions, open iPhone Settings, find Yiji, and enable microphone, speech recognition, and notifications.

2. Why did my reminder not appear?

  • Check that notification permission is enabled
  • Make sure the reminder time is in the future
  • Make sure iPhone notifications are allowed for Yiji

Create a reminder 2–3 minutes in the future and lock the iPhone to verify actual local notification delivery.

3. Why is weather missing from the calendar?

Calendar weather requires When In Use location permission and a working network connection. Denying location disables only weather; capture, search, reminders, and backups remain available. You can re-enable location in iPhone Settings and refresh the calendar.

4. How do backups work?

  • Tap “Generate Backup File” to export local data
  • Save or share the backup through the iOS Files flow, including iCloud Drive if you want to protect data before deleting the app or move it manually to another device
  • Tap “Import Backup File” to restore a previously exported JSON backup

Importing replaces current local records and reminders, and local notifications are re-synced after import. This release does not automatically sync two devices; export and import a backup manually when moving data.

5. Does the app upload my data to a developer server?

The current release does not include account registration or automatic cloud sync, and does not send your record content, reminder content, or search history to a developer-operated server. Voice recognition depends on iOS system capabilities, and related system-level processing may be subject to Apple's privacy policies.
