## 中文版

### 欢迎使用易记

易记是一款本地优先的生活记录工具，帮助你用一句自然语言快速记住：

- 物品放在哪里
- 什么时候需要提醒

### 当前版本支持

- 文字录入
- 语音录入
- 物品位置记录
- 提醒事项记录
- 普通笔记记录
- 本地搜索
- 本地提醒通知
- 本地备份导出与导入

### 当前版本限制

- 当前版本最多保存 `30` 条记录
- 暂不支持账号、云同步、group 和付费升级
- 启动页最终品牌素材仍会在后续版本替换

### 常见问题

#### 1. 为什么点击语音后没有反应？

请先检查：

- 是否已允许麦克风权限
- 是否已允许语音识别权限
- 是否在真机上测试

如需重新授权：

1. 打开 iPhone `设置`
2. 找到 `易记`
3. 开启麦克风、语音识别和通知权限

#### 2. 为什么提醒没有弹出来？

请先检查：

- 是否已允许通知权限
- 提醒时间是否设置在未来
- iPhone 是否开启了系统通知

你也可以在应用的“我的”页面中发送一条 `10` 秒后的测试通知，用来确认本地通知链路是否正常。

#### 3. 为什么不能继续新增记录？

当前版本对本地记录数量设置了上限：

- 免费阶段最多保存 `30` 条记录

达到上限后，应用会阻止继续新增。  
如果需要继续使用，可先删除旧记录，或导出备份后整理内容。

#### 4. 备份文件如何使用？

在“我的”页面中：

- 点击“生成备份文件”导出当前本地数据
- 点击“导入备份文件”恢复之前导出的 JSON 文件

请注意：

- 导入会覆盖当前本地记录和提醒
- 导入后应用会重新同步本地通知
- 如果准备删除 App，仍建议先导出一份备份文件

#### 5. 我的数据会上传到开发者服务器吗？

当前首发版本不提供账号系统，也不把记录内容上传到开发者自建服务器；当 iCloud 可用时，数据会同步到你自己的私有 iCloud 数据库。
语音识别能力依赖 iOS 系统能力，相关系统级处理可能受 Apple 隐私政策约束。

## English Version

### Welcome to Yiji

Yiji is a local-first memory app that helps you quickly save everyday information with a single natural sentence:

- where you put things
- when you need a reminder
- notes you want to remember later

### Features in the Current Version

- Text input
- Voice input
- Storage/location records
- Reminder records
- General note records
- Local search
- Local reminder notifications
- Private iCloud sync and local backup export / import

### Current Limitations

- The current version supports up to `30` saved records
- No account system, group sharing, or paid upgrade yet
- Final launch screen branding assets will be updated in a later revision

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### 1. Why does voice input not start?

Please check:

- microphone permission is enabled
- speech recognition permission is enabled
- you are testing on a real iPhone

To re-enable permissions:

1. Open iPhone `Settings`
2. Find `Yiji`
3. Enable microphone, speech recognition, and notifications

#### 2. Why did my reminder not appear?

Please check:

- notification permission is enabled
- the reminder time is in the future
- iPhone notifications are allowed for Yiji

You can also send a `10`-second test notification from the Settings page inside the app to verify local notification delivery.

#### 3. Why can I no longer add new records?

The current version has a local record limit:

- up to `30` saved records

Once the limit is reached, the app blocks additional saves.  
You can delete older records or export a backup before cleaning up data.

#### 4. How do backups work?

From the Settings page:

- tap “Generate Backup File” to export local data
- save or share the backup through the iOS Files flow, including iCloud Drive if you want to move it to another device
- tap “Import Backup File” to restore a previously exported JSON backup

Please note:

- importing replaces current local records and reminders
- local notifications are re-synced after import
- manual backups remain the safest protection before deleting the app

#### 5. Does the app upload my data to a developer server?

The current release does not include account registration, and data is primarily stored locally on your device while syncing to your private iCloud database when available.
Voice recognition depends on iOS system capabilities, and related system-level processing may be subject to Apple's privacy policies.
