## 中文版

### 当前版本支持

- 文字录入
- 语音录入
- 物品位置记录
- 提醒事项记录
- 普通笔记记录
- 本地搜索
- 本地提醒通知
- 日历天气提示（需要用户授权定位）
- 本地备份导出与导入，可通过“文件”或 iCloud Drive 手动传递

### 当前版本限制

- 暂不支持账号、group、订阅和付费升级
- 当前版本不提供自动云同步，跨设备使用请手动导出和导入备份
- 界面支持简体中文、繁体中文、English、Español 与日本語
- 语音识别语言可独立选择跟随 App、`zh-CN`、`zh-TW`、`en-US`、`es-US`、`es-MX`、`es-ES` 或 `ja-JP`
- 语音识别选择“跟随 App”时，五种界面语言分别使用 `zh-CN`、`zh-TW`、`en-US`、`es-US` 与 `ja-JP`
- 当前版本中，中文、英文、西班牙语和日语都可用于语音输入。中文、西班牙语和日语支持常用提醒时间、重复规则、物品位置记录与自然语言位置搜索；英文支持语音/文字笔记和关键词搜索。

### 如何切换语言？

打开Mind Talk的“设置”页，在“语言”中分别选择 App 语言和语音识别语言。App 语言可选“跟随系统”“简体中文”“繁体中文”“English”“Español”或“日本語”；语音识别可选择跟随 App，也可单独指定中国大陆普通话、台湾中文、美国英语、美国西班牙语、墨西哥西班牙语、西班牙西班牙语或日语（日本）。

### 常见问题

#### 1. 为什么点击语音后没有反应？

请先检查：

- 是否已允许麦克风权限
- 是否已允许语音识别权限
- 是否在真机上测试

如需重新授权：

1. 打开 iPhone `设置`
2. 找到 `Mind Talk`
3. 开启麦克风、语音识别和通知权限

#### 2. 为什么提醒没有弹出来？

请先检查：

- 是否已允许通知权限
- 提醒时间是否设置在未来
- iPhone 是否开启了系统通知

可新建一条 2～3 分钟后的提醒并锁屏等待，用真实提醒验证本地通知链路。

#### 3. 为什么日历没有天气？

日历天气需要“使用 App 时”定位权限和可用网络。拒绝定位只会关闭天气提示，不影响记录、搜索、提醒或备份。可到 iPhone“设置”中重新授权后返回日历刷新。

#### 4. 备份文件如何使用？

在“设置”页面中：

- 点击“生成备份文件”导出当前本地数据
- 如需更换设备或删除 App，建议先把备份文件保存到“文件”App 或 iCloud Drive
- 点击“导入备份文件”恢复之前导出的 JSON 文件

请注意：

- 导入会覆盖当前本地记录和提醒
- 导入后应用会重新同步本地通知

#### 5. 我的数据会上传到开发者服务器吗？

当前首发版本不提供账号系统或自动云同步，也不把记录内容上传到开发者自建服务器。记录保存在本机；只有在你主动导出备份后，文件才会保存到你选择的位置。
语音识别能力依赖 iOS 系统能力，相关系统级处理可能受 Apple 隐私政策约束。

## English Version

### Features in the Current Version

- Text input
- Voice input
- Storage/location records
- Reminder records
- General note records
- Local search
- Local reminder notifications
- Optional calendar weather with location permission
- Local backup export and import, with manual transfer through Files or iCloud Drive

### Current Limitations

- No account system, group sharing, subscription, or paid upgrade
- No automatic cloud sync in the current release; use manual backup export and import across devices
- The interface supports English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Spanish, and Japanese
- Speech recognition can follow the app language or be set independently to `zh-CN`, `zh-TW`, `en-US`, `es-US`, `es-MX`, `es-ES`, or `ja-JP`
- In the current release, Chinese, English, Spanish, and Japanese can all be used for voice input
- Chinese, Spanish, and Japanese support common reminder schedules, repeat rules, item-location capture, and natural-language location search; English supports voice and text notes plus keyword search

### How do I change the language?

Open Settings in Mind Talk and choose the app language and speech recognition language separately. The app can follow the system or use Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, Spanish, or Japanese. Speech recognition can follow the app or use `zh-CN`, `zh-TW`, `en-US`, `es-US`, `es-MX`, `es-ES`, or `ja-JP` independently.

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### 1. Why does voice input not start?

Please check:

- microphone permission is enabled
- speech recognition permission is enabled
- you are testing on a real iPhone

To re-enable permissions:

1. Open iPhone `Settings`
2. Find `Mind Talk`
3. Enable microphone, speech recognition, and notifications

#### 2. Why did my reminder not appear?

Please check:

- notification permission is enabled
- the reminder time is in the future
- iPhone notifications are allowed for Mind Talk

Create a reminder 2–3 minutes in the future and lock the iPhone to verify actual local notification delivery.

#### 3. Why is weather missing from the calendar?

Calendar weather requires When In Use location permission and a working network connection. Denying location disables only weather; capture, search, reminders, and backups remain available. You can re-enable location in iPhone Settings and refresh the calendar.

#### 4. How do backups work?

From the Settings page:

- tap “Generate Backup File” to export local data
- save the file to Files or iCloud Drive before deleting the app or moving data to another device
- tap “Import Backup File” to restore a previously exported JSON backup

Please note:

- importing replaces current local records and reminders
- local notifications are re-synced after import

#### 5. Does the app upload my data to a developer server?

The current release does not include account registration or developer-operated cloud sync. Records stay on your device unless you explicitly export a backup to a location you choose.
Voice recognition depends on iOS system capabilities, and related system-level processing may be subject to Apple's privacy policies.
