1. 我们是谁

易记是一款面向个人生活场景的本地优先记录应用，用于帮助用户记录物品位置、提醒事项和临时笔记。

2. 我们收集什么信息

当前版本不要求你创建账号，也不主动向开发者服务器上传你的个人记录内容。

  • 你手动输入的记录内容
  • 你通过语音转写后确认保存的记录内容
  • 你创建的提醒事项与提醒时间
  • 最近搜索历史
  • 你手动导出的本地备份文件内容

这些信息默认保存在你的设备本地；当你在设备上启用了 iCloud 时，相关记录、提醒和搜索历史也可能同步到你自己的私有 iCloud 数据库，用于实现跨设备恢复与同步。

3. 语音与麦克风权限

当你使用语音录入功能时，易记会请求麦克风权限和语音识别权限。

这些权限仅用于将你的语音转换为文字并填入应用内的录入内容。当前版本不提供开发者自建语音服务器， 不会主动把你的语音录音上传到开发者控制的后端。

iOS 语音识别能力可能由 Apple 提供或处理，你对该功能的使用也会受到 Apple 自身隐私政策和系统行为的约束。

4. 通知权限

当你使用提醒功能时，易记会请求通知权限，用于在提醒时间到达时，通过 iPhone 的本地通知向你发送提醒。

当前版本使用的是本地通知，不依赖开发者服务器推送。

5. 文件访问与本地备份

当你导入备份文件时，易记会通过系统文件选择器访问你主动选中的 JSON 文件。导出备份文件时，易记会在系统提供的分享与存储流程中生成备份文件。

易记不会在未获得你主动操作的情况下，浏览或读取你的其他文件。

6. 数据共享

  • 不提供账号系统
  • 支持同步到用户自己的私有 iCloud 数据库
  • 不提供群组共享
  • 不提供广告 SDK
  • 不提供第三方营销跟踪
  • 不向开发者服务器发送你的记录正文、提醒内容或搜索历史

7. 数据保留与删除

你的记录、提醒和搜索历史默认保留在你的设备本地，直到你在应用内手动删除、用导入备份覆盖当前数据，或删除应用。

如果你在设备上启用了 iCloud，同步后的副本也可能继续保留在你的私有 iCloud 账户中，并在你重新安装后重新下载到设备。

如果你导出了备份文件，该备份文件的保存、删除和转移由你自己控制。

8. 儿童隐私

当前版本不面向儿童进行定向画像、广告投放或社交运营。如果你是监护人，并认为未成年人在未经允许的情况下使用了本应用，请通过支持渠道联系开发者。

9. 政策更新

如果易记未来进一步引入账号、家庭 group、开发者服务器接口或第三方服务，本隐私政策将同步更新。

English

1. Who We Are

Yiji is a local-first personal memory app designed to help users save everyday information such as where items are stored, what reminders they need, and quick notes.

2. What Information We Collect

In the current version, Yiji does not require account registration and does not actively upload your personal records to a developer-operated server.

  • Text that you manually enter
  • Text saved after voice transcription
  • Reminder titles, times, and statuses
  • Recent search history
  • Backup data that you choose to export manually

This information is used to provide local record storage, search, reminders, backup restore, and, when iCloud is enabled on your device, synchronization through your private iCloud database.

3. Microphone and Speech Recognition Permissions

When you use voice input, Yiji may request microphone permission and speech recognition permission.

These permissions are used only to convert your speech into text inside the app. Yiji does not currently operate its own speech-processing backend and does not intentionally upload your audio recordings to a developer-controlled server.

However, iOS speech recognition may rely on Apple-provided services or system processing, and your use of that functionality is also subject to Apple's own privacy policies and system behavior.

4. Notification Permission

When you use reminders, Yiji may request notification permission so your iPhone can deliver local reminder notifications at the scheduled time.

The current version uses local notifications and does not rely on developer server push delivery.

5. File Access and Local Backups

If you import a backup, Yiji accesses only the JSON file that you explicitly choose through the system file picker. If you export a backup, Yiji creates a backup file through the standard iOS share and save flow, which may include saving the file to Files or iCloud Drive if you choose.

Yiji does not browse or read unrelated files on your device without your direct action.

6. Data Sharing

  • No account registration
  • Optional sync to the user's private iCloud database
  • No group sharing
  • No advertising SDKs
  • No third-party marketing trackers
  • No record text, reminder text, or search history sent to a developer-operated backend

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it manually inside the app, overwrite it by importing a backup, or remove the app from your device.

If iCloud is enabled on your device, a synced copy may also remain in your private iCloud account and may download again after reinstalling the app.

If you export a backup file, storage and deletion of that file are under your control.

8. Children's Privacy

The current version is not designed for profiling children, targeted advertising, or social interaction. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a minor used the app inappropriately, please contact the developer through the support channel.

9. Policy Updates

If Yiji later introduces accounts, family groups, developer-operated server-side services, or third-party integrations, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.
